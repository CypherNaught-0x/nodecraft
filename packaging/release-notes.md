> **The macOS and Windows builds are not signed yet** (#100), so the first launch shows a warning:
>
> - **macOS:** open the app once, click **Done**, then **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway**.
> - **Windows:** on *Windows protected your PC*, click **More info › Run anyway**.
>
> Step-by-step instructions for every platform: [docs/install.md](https://github.com/CypherNaught-0x/nodecraft/blob/main/docs/install.md). Checksums of every file are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
