## 2026-10-23 · Presence in shared projects (#120)

Everyone in a shared project sees where the others are: their pointer and name, the cards they
have selected, and an initial per person beside the Share chip.

**Presence is not part of the project.** It is never written to the yrs document, saved, undone
or audited. Each peer sends a `Presence` frame (the open canvas, the pointer in canvas points,
up to 256 selected card ids). The relay keeps each peer's latest and sends it to the others as a
`Person`. It tells a newcomer where everyone already is, and sends `Gone` when a peer that was
shown leaves or is put out. `PROTOCOL` went from 3 to 4, so an older app and a newer relay refuse
each other at the hello instead of misreading frames.

**The relay names people; nobody names themselves.** A link holder is shown by the name on its
link's `Grant`, which the owner chose. Whoever came in by the room id is shown as "Owner". There
is no display-name setting. A self-declared name would let anyone in the room pose as someone
else, and a later account system can replace "Owner" without a protocol change. The `peer`
number tells two people of the same name apart, and picks their colour.

**Every role may say where it is.** A viewer's pointer is as useful to the others as an editor's.
It changes nothing in the project.

**Bounded both ends.** A presence with a non-finite pointer, a pointer more than 10⁷ points from
the origin, more than 256 selected cards, or a pointer or selection without a canvas fails to
decode, and the relay hangs up on whoever sent it. The app sends at most every 50 ms and only
when something changed. The relay drops presence frames that come faster than every 25 ms. A
session keeps at most 64 people. Incoming presence doesn't make the app settle the document
again; it only redraws.
