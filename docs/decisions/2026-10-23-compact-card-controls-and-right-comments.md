## 2026-10-23 · Compact card controls and right-side discussions (#902)

- Ordinary cards derive deterministic world metrics from the control kinds they actually draw, plus theme padding, preview/answer regions and compact history rows. The former 220-point minimum plus 64 points per parameter overallocated short source/action cards. The shared `card::world_size`/`world_rect` path still supplies ports, wires, culling, fit, fields and auto-format; widget measurements never mutate geometry after painting. Conditional controls reserve their declared space to keep parameter edits from moving wires; composer prompts and hidden workflow-model fields reserve no duplicate row. Semantic count roles owned by the composer still retain conservative parameter space until admitted roles are propagated through every shared geometry consumer; this nonblocking follow-up is tracked in [#907](https://github.com/CypherNaught-0x/nodecraft/issues/907) rather than guessed from parameter names. Long bodies scroll at the existing cap. Generation composer, streaming answer and speech-preview reservations remain deliberate, not accidental whitespace. Image actions reserve their declared image-output preview before a first live result, so a drag preview cannot consume the parameter area or change card geometry; PDF source first-page previews now reserve this same region explicitly. The 2-point `control_vertical_padding` token keeps native widget heights consistent with the deterministic rows.
- Slider tracks and their separate exact-value inputs fill the card's content width; choice buttons and text editors use the same width. Drag previews remain local and publish one command on release; exact numeric drafts retain existing validation, Enter/blur commit and Escape cancellation. The row remains present on release so control geometry does not jump during editing. Compact multi-port cards retain a logical height floor for their port rows; vertically partitioned hit targets keep adjacent ports independently reachable even at overview zoom or during additive legacy-input previews.
- The Comments button and panel move to the viewport right, below search and above bottom navigation. Tags retain the left side; concurrent panels share narrow viewports. Node-attached markers that intersect card decorations or the output hit area project beside the card's right edge. Safe explicit off-card offsets and canvas pins are not relocated. All projection is view state: existing saved anchors, undo and collaboration data are unchanged. The selected panel-occluded marker retains its leader and drag-offset semantics; cramped viewports may require this visibility exception instead of the ordinary right-of-card placement.
- Quick-created nodes remain centered under the pointer using actual resolved instance geometry, rather than the old minimum height. Existing import/drop/library cascade origins remain approximate placement hints: they did not exactly center their heterogeneous preview/schema bodies before this change, and exposing importer admission just to measure them is outside this view-layout correction.

This supersedes the fixed parameter-row and full history-row sizing described in [the initial history decision](2026-10-18-generation-history-on-the-card.md), without changing any history commands or data. See [UI design](../ui-design.md) for current tokens and interactions.
